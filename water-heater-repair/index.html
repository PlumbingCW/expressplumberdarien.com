What to do right now if the heater is failing

Some tank problems can wait for a morning appointment. These cannot.

  • ✓
    Water is pooling at the baseShut the cold supply, cut power or gas, and put a bucket or towels down. A leak from the tank shell is rarely a repair, but a leak from a fitting or valve often is.
  • ✓
    You smell gas or rotten eggs near the heaterLeave and call 911 or the gas utility. Do not light anything, and do not try to relight a pilot.
  • ✓
    The relief valve is running or the pipe is very hotOpen a hot faucet to relieve pressure, keep people away from the discharge pipe, and call right away.
  • ✓
    Scorched smell, soot or a yellow flameTurn the heater off and ventilate. Poor combustion on a gas unit needs a technician before the next use.

No hot water with a dry tank and no smells is usually urgent in winter but not dangerous. Tell dispatch which case you have and we will schedule it the right way.

Why a tank water heater stops making hot water

Most no-hot-water calls come down to a short list of parts. On a gas unit it is often the thermocouple, the flame sensor, the gas control valve or a dirty burner. On an electric unit it is usually a heating element, a thermostat or a tripped high-limit switch.

Your first check costs nothing. Look at the breaker for an electric heater, or at the pilot window for a gas heater. If the breaker trips again right after you reset it, stop resetting it. Repeated trips point to a shorted element, and that needs a meter, not another reset.

Sediment, noise and the anode rod

Tank water leaves minerals and grit on the bottom. Over time that layer insulates the water from the burner or element, so the unit works harder and starts to pop. Flushing helps when the heater is still healthy.

The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the tank steel does not. Once it is gone, the tank itself starts to rust. Darien buys treated Lake Michigan water from the City of Chicago, but scale and sediment still build up in any tank. For a hardness number, ask the City water operator at (630) 353-8105 or test your tap.

When repair stops making sense

A repair is a good bet when the tank is sound and the failed part is modest. It is a poor bet when the shell leaks, when the tank has rusted from the inside, or when the same unit has needed repairs back to back.

Ask the plumber to read the rating plate with you. It shows the capacity, fuel type and a serial number that usually encodes the manufacture date. If the unit is old, a larger repair often buys little. Our water heater replacement page explains that path, and tankless units are an option if you want to rethink the whole setup.

Permits and licensing

Illinois requires a license for anyone who installs, alters, repairs or maintains plumbing, and you can check a plumber with the state's public license verification tool. Repairs should follow the manufacturer's installation guidelines for the model on your rating plate. Swapping a part is a repair. Changing the heater or its piping may need a permit from the City of Darien Community Development Department, so ask them before the work starts.
