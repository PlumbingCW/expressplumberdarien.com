What we do

We handle the calls people dread: burst pipes, sewer backups, failed water heaters, flooding basements and toilets that will not stop running. We also schedule planned work such as water heater installs, repiping and sump pump replacement.

Every call begins with a conversation. Dispatch asks what you see, tells you what to shut off and books the earliest appointment available. The plumber gives a clear price before work starts.

Emergency calls and planned work

Some jobs cannot wait. A split pipe, a sewer backup, a failed sump pump in a storm or a water heater leaking onto the floor are treated as emergencies, and dispatch books them first.

Other jobs can be scheduled. Water heater installs, whole house repiping, sewer camera inspections and water softener setup are planned around your calendar. Our services page groups both kinds so you can find the right fit.

If you are unsure which category your problem is in, call and describe it. Dispatch will tell you honestly whether it can wait for a regular appointment.

Licensing and permits

Illinois requires a license for anyone who installs, alters, repairs or maintains plumbing. You can ask about this on the call, and the State of Illinois offers a public license verification tool if you want to check before a plumber arrives.

Plumbing work in Illinois is held to the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). Permits for plumbing changes in Darien come from the City's Community Development Department at 1702 Plainfield Road. Routine clearing of a clog does not usually change the plumbing, but repairs and replacements often do, and we will tell you what applies to your job.
