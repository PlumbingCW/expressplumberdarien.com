Plumbing installation means adding or relocating anything that carries water in or waste out: a toilet, a vanity, a dishwasher, a utility sink or a whole new bathroom. Planned installs are scheduled by appointment, and our 24/7 line at (630) 822-9290 is also open if a project hits a surprise at night.

Before the visit, decide where the fixture will go and have the model number in hand. Many fixtures have rough-in dimensions that decide where the pipes must stop. Photos of the current layout, plus the spot where you want the new one, help dispatch send the right parts.

What we install, and what sits behind each one

Every fixture has three needs: clean water coming in, waste going out and a vent that lets the drain breathe. A fixture that skips any one of them will gurgle, drain slowly or smell.

The visible part is the easy part. The real work is the pipe path inside the wall and under the floor, and whether the existing lines have room for one more fixture.

Remodels in a 1960s-era city

Darien was incorporated in 1969 by residents of the Marion Hills, Brookhaven, Farmingdale and Hinsbrook subdivisions, and many homes date from the 1960s onward. That matters for a remodel because older houses often have smaller drain lines and fewer vents than a new bathroom needs.

So before you buy a double vanity or a freestanding tub, let a plumber check what the existing lines can carry. Sometimes the answer is a simple tie-in. Sometimes a branch has to be upsized, and knowing that early keeps your tile order from landing on top of a surprise.

Gas lines: leave this to a licensed pro

Gas range, dryer, fireplace log and water heater connections are not a weekend project. Illinois requires a license for plumbing work, and a gas joint that is slightly off can leak without any visible sign.

  • ✓
    If you smell gasLeave the building at once. Do not flip switches, use a phone inside or light anything. Call 911 from outside, then call your gas utility.
  • ✓
    Never test with a flameSoapy water and a calibrated tester are the safe ways to find a leak. A lighter is not.
  • ✓
    New appliance, new connectionA gas range or dryer needs the right connector, shutoff valve and a pressure test at the joint before it is used.
  • ✓
    Not sure what you smellA faint odor near an appliance is still worth a call. The odor added to gas is meant to be noticed early.

If your project includes a gas appliance swap, our water heater installation page covers gas and vent connections in more detail.

What shapes the price of an install

Two bathrooms with the same fixtures can cost different amounts. These are the factors that move the price.

Distance to existing linesA vanity back to back with an existing wall is simple. One across the room needs more pipe and more wall opening.

AccessAn open basement ceiling below makes runs easier than a finished one.
Drain and vent changesUpsizing a drain or adding a vent stack is its own task.

A newly installed fixture that leaks later is usually a covered-cause question under your homeowners policy, depending on the policy. Keep the invoice and photos of the finished work.

Permits and the license on your job

Plumbing changes in Darien need a permit. The City of Darien Community Development Department at 1702 Plainfield Rd handles permits, and plumbing contractors file their State of Illinois license with the City's contractor registration. Ask Community Development what applies to your project before work starts. Our work follows the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code), and we follow each fixture maker's installation instructions.

A like-for-like swap, such as replacing a faucet, is different from adding a new line, so describe the work when you call. If you also want your supply lines evaluated at the same time, see whole house repiping or have a leak detection visit first.
