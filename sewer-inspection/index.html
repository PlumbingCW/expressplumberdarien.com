A sewer camera inspection sends a small waterproof camera through the building drain and sewer lateral, so you can see roots, cracks, offsets and sagging sections without digging. Call (630) 822-9290 to schedule. Our line is open 24/7, so you can book outside business hours too.

To prepare, make sure the cleanout is reachable and keep water use low during the visit. If the line is currently backing up, treat it as urgent and call right away. A blocked line may need clearing before the camera can pass.

What a camera can and cannot tell you

The camera shows pipe material, joints, cracks, root entry, standing water and breaks. It can tell you where along the line a problem sits by measuring the distance from the cleanout.

It cannot see through a full pipe, and it will not tell you the exact cause of every slow drain in the house. If the line is packed with debris, we clear it first. The inspection is a look at the condition of the pipe, not a repair. We run the camera within the manufacturer's guidelines for cable and head, so we do not force it through a damaged pipe.

Good times to book an inspection

You do not need a backup to justify a look. These are the situations where the video usually pays for itself.

  • ✓
    Before buying a homeA sewer line is expensive to repair and invisible from the surface. Video lets you negotiate with facts.
  • ✓
    After repeat clogsA line that keeps stopping may have a root mass or a low section that cleaning cannot fix.
  • ✓
    Before a remodel or yard projectKnow where the lateral runs and what shape it is in before you pour concrete over it.
  • ✓
    When you see lawn dips or damp spotsSoil settling above the sewer path can signal a leak underground.

Darien housing age and what to look for

Darien incorporated in 1969, and the Marion Hills, Brookhaven, Farmingdale and Hinsbrook subdivisions grew up through the 1960s. Houses of that period may have original clay tile or cast iron lines, and the joints are where roots enter.

If you are buying in one of those areas, a camera look at the lateral is a sensible step. It tells you the pipe's condition today, which is more useful than guessing from the house's age.

Pairing the camera with other work

Many people book the camera together with cleaning. If the video shows a blockage, sewer line cleaning clears it. If grease or scale coats the wall, hydro jetting scours it, and a second pass of video confirms the result.
