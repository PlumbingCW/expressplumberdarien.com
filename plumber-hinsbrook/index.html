Who to call when the water is not coming from your pipes

Plumbing trouble in a basement is not always a plumbing problem. Cass Avenue, 75th Street and Plainfield Road are DuPage County roads, so standing water on them is a county matter. The City of Darien's storm notice lists a county number for overland flooding on those roads, (630) 407-6920.

Water on a city street or in a detention basin goes to Municipal Services at (630) 353-8105. Water coming up through a drain in your house is a different call, and that is where a plumber helps. Sorting out which one you have saves a long wait on the wrong line.

What is known about Hinsbrook homes, and what is not

One real-estate guide describes Hinsbrook as single-family housing from the late 1960s. That fits the 1969 incorporation date. It tells you the house is older than many of the fixtures in it, since faucets, toilets and water heaters get replaced over the years.

It does not tell you what pipe material sits behind your walls. Repairs and remodels over decades change that house by house. Ask a plumber to identify what you have, using the shutoff, the water heater connections and the basement drain, rather than assuming anything from the build year.

Sewer and drain responsibility around your house

The City's sewer page says the sewer from the curb to the house belongs to the homeowner. The street sewer belongs to the City, a township or the county. That line decides who pays for what.

Water heaters, leaks and sump pumps in older single-family houses

Water heaters are among the most common fixtures to age out in any older house. A tank with moisture at its base, rusty hot water or a pilot that keeps going out is worth a look, and our water heater replacement page explains the sizing and removal steps.

Hidden leaks show up as a water bill that rises with no change in use. Leak detection starts with the meter test above. If your pit pump is old or has not run all year, sump pump repair and a test run before storm season are a sensible precaution against a wet floor.

Permits matter here. Illinois requires a license for plumbing work, and Darien's Community Development Department at 1702 Plainfield Road issues permits when plumbing is changed. Work is held to the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code).
