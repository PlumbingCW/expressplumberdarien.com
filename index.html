Five steps that limit the damage

Gas smell is a different call

If you smell gas, do not call us first. Leave the house, then call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not flip switches or use a phone indoors. A plumber can help with gas piping after the utility has made the area safe.

Darien homes

Why plumbing problems look the way they do in Darien

Subdivisions from the 1960s and the pipes under them

Darien became a city in 1969 when residents of Marion Hills, Brookhaven, Farmingdale and Hinsbrook joined together. Many homes in these neighborhoods date from the 1960s and 1970s, which means older drain materials, aging water heaters and basements that depend on a working sump pump.

In practice, that is why we hear so much about floor drains that gurgle, water heaters past their prime and finished basements that flood after a hard rain. If your home is from that era, a camera look at the sewer line and a check of the sump pump are worth doing before trouble starts.

Waterfall Glen Forest Preserve runs along the south edge of the city, and heavy rain moves through the area quickly. When the ground is saturated, sump pumps work harder, and a pump that has not been tested in a year can fail exactly when it is needed.

Water, permits and who to ask

Darien buys treated Lake Michigan water from the City of Chicago. If you have a question about your water service, the City of Darien water operator can be reached at (630) 353-8105.

Plumbing changes in Darien go through the City's Community Development Department at 1702 Plainfield Road, and Illinois requires a state license for plumbing work. We can tell you whether a job you have in mind needs a permit when you call.
