Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries all household waste out to the city sewer, usually through a cleanout near the basement wall or in the yard. If your sewer is backing up in Darien, call (630) 822-9290. The emergency line is open 24/7, including nights and weekends.

Until we arrive, do not run the dishwasher, washer, showers or sinks, and do not flush. Water you add has nowhere to go but the lowest drain in the house. If a floor drain has already come up, keep people out of that room and wear gloves for any cleanup.

Signs the main line is blocked

A main line problem shows up in more than one place. One slow sink is usually local. Several symptoms at once usually are not.

Watch for a toilet that bubbles when you run the washer, a basement floor drain that fills or smells, and showers that drain slowly on the lowest floor. A drain that works in the morning and fails after laundry day often means the line is partly blocked and the load tips it over.

How a main line cleaning goes

  1. 1

    Find the cleanout

    We locate the access point and open it carefully. Sewer gas and standing water mean we work with the line under control.

  2. 2

    Run the cable or jetter

    The machine goes toward the street until the blockage breaks and water moves.

  3. 3

    Flow test

    We run water at several fixtures to confirm the line is open end to end.

  4. 4

    Camera pass

    A camera checks the pipe for roots, cracks and low spots so the clog is not just treated, it is understood.

The takeaway for a homeowner is to keep a record of how often the line backs up. Two or three backups in a few years is a signal to ask for the camera video, because cleaning only treats the symptom if the pipe is roughened or cracked.

Keeping the line clear afterward

Prevention is mostly habit. Put only toilet paper and waste in the toilet. Wipes labeled flushable still do not break down like paper. Keep grease out of the kitchen sink, and scrape plates into the trash instead of the disposal. Cleanout access on the building drain is something the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code) addresses, which is one reason we do not cut corners on it.

If trees stand near the sewer path, roots may return. A yearly or twice-yearly jetting schedule is a common choice for lines with root history. Our hydro jetting service is built for that kind of maintenance.

When a stormy night makes things worse

A line that only backs up during heavy rain points to a different problem than a plain clog. The City of Darien has told residents to keep sump pump discharge off the sanitary sewer, and anyone with questions can call (630) 353-8105.

If your sump discharges into a household drain, that water adds load to the same pipe we are clearing. Our sump pump service can reroute the discharge outdoors.
