Boiler repair means finding and fixing the failed part in a hydronic heating system, which heats water and sends it through pipes to radiators or baseboard. If your boiler has stopped in Darien, call (630) 822-9290. Our line is open 24/7, which matters when the house is cooling on a winter night.

While you wait, check that the thermostat is calling for heat, the boiler switch is on, and the gas supply valve is open. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. If the boiler is leaking, shut its water feed valve and switch it off.

Start with pressure, water and power

Most boiler checks begin with three gauges and switches. The pressure gauge should sit in the range the label shows. The water level should be right. The power switch and thermostat should be on.

Do not keep adding water if the pressure keeps falling. Falling pressure means water is going somewhere, such as a leak, a failed relief valve or a stuck feed valve. That needs a plumber.

Circulator pumps, zone valves and trapped air

If the boiler runs but rooms stay cold, the heated water may not be moving. The circulator pump pushes water around the loop, and a failed motor or seized shaft stops it. Zone valves open and close heat to different parts of the house, and a stuck one can leave a zone cold.

Trapped air also blocks flow. Gurgling in the pipes and a cold radiator top are typical clues. Air can often be bled, but a system that keeps filling with air has a leak or a failing expansion tank somewhere.

Leaks: small drips and real failures

A weeping valve packing or a drip at a union is often a repair. A leak from the heat exchanger or the boiler body is different, because those parts are not repaired in the field in most cases. Unexplained wet floors near the boiler can also come from the piping around it, and our leak detection service can trace those.

A relief valve that discharges is a warning, not a nuisance. It means pressure or temperature went past the limit. Keep clear of the discharge pipe and call.

Repair, maintain or replace

A repair makes sense when the boiler is otherwise sound and the failed part is modest. It is a weaker choice when the heat exchanger has cracked, when repairs repeat, or when the unit is far past its rated life. Ask for the rating plate details and the manufacture date.

Boilers are often tied to the home's domestic hot water as well. If yours feeds an indirect tank, a water heater repair visit may cover the tank side of the same system.

Licensing and permits

Illinois requires a license for anyone who repairs or alters plumbing, and boiler piping work falls under the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). Gas and venting changes can carry added requirements, so ask the City of Darien Community Development Department what applies before a boiler is replaced or its piping is changed.
