When a Downers Grove plumbing problem cannot wait

Some problems can sit until morning. These four cannot, and each one is a reason to call the emergency line right away.

  • ✓
    Water you cannot stopA broken supply pipe, a failed washing machine hose or a pipe that let go in a wall keeps flowing until the main is closed. Shut the main and call.
  • ✓
    Sewage coming upDark water rising in a basement floor drain, tub or shower points to the main line. Stop using every fixture and call.
  • ✓
    A leaking or dead water heaterWater at the base of a tank, or a tank that has stopped heating with a puddle nearby, needs attention the same day.
  • ✓
    A gas smellLeave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Plumbers do not work on a suspected gas leak until the utility clears it.

A dripping faucet or a slow tub drain is a normal appointment. Tell dispatch which situation you have and we will route the call the right way.

What the Village's own records say about your pipes

The Village of Downers Grove says homes built before 1930 most likely have a lead water service line, while homes built after 1960 are likely to have copper. It also says all 233 miles of Village water mains are iron or PVC. Check your house age against those ranges before you plan any pipe work.

The line is split in two. The Village owns the public side, from the main to the shutoff near the property line. You own the private side from there to the house. A leak on your side is your repair, so leak detection or water service line repair is the right call, not a report to the Village.

The Village also says a lead line replacement program will not begin until after November 15, 2027, and replacement is strongly recommended but not required. If you want to replace your line sooner for a different reason, ask the Village first so the work is recorded correctly.

Heavy rain, low spots and the Village flood guidance

A damaging 2006 storm caused severe flooding across Downers Grove. In response, the Village adopted a Watershed Infrastructure Improvement Plan that named 16 high-priority drainage areas. Washington Park was the first major project, and its new stormwater basins hold runoff during intense rains.

The Village also describes Localized Poor Drainage Areas, which are bowl-shaped low spots where runoff collects. If your lot is in one, a working sump pump and a clear discharge line matter more than they would on higher ground. See our sump pump repair and basement waterproofing pages.

Know the limits of Village help. Its stormwater page says the Village does not pump out basements, and it may pump flooded yards only case by case. For a true flooding emergency it says to dial 911. Standard homeowners and renters policies generally do not cover flood, so call your insurer early.
