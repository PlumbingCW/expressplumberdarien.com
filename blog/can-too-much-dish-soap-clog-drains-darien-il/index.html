Can Too Much Dish Soap Clog Your Drains?

A little extra soap rarely blocks a pipe. Foam, grease and soap scum are another story, and they explain most of the sink problems blamed on dish soap.

The short answer

No, dish soap by itself does not clog a drain. It is a liquid that rinses away with water, and a normal sink or dishwasher load will carry it through the line without leaving much behind. Pouring extra soap down a drain on purpose is not a recipe for a plug.

But soap can help build one. Mixed with grease, food scraps and minerals, it forms a gummy film that clings to the inside of pipes. Too much soap in a dishwasher, a disposal or a washing machine also creates foam that has nowhere to go. That foam is often what looks like a clog.

What dish soap really does inside a pipe

Soap is made to break up grease so it rinses off a plate. That job does not stop at the drain.

These are general patterns. A plumber confirms the actual cause in your line.

Where grease fits in

A line that clogs after dish soap use almost always has grease in it. Soap suspends grease in warm water. When that water reaches a cooler stretch of pipe, the grease separates and sticks. Over weeks, the layer thickens until water barely passes.

That is why a drain can seem fine for months and then stop in one evening. The soap did not cause it. The soap helped carry the grease to a place where it could set. Cooking fat belongs in a can in the trash, not in the sink, with or without soap.

What to do if your sink is slow or full of suds

  1. 1

    Stop adding water

    Let the sink or machine sit. More soap and more water only deepen the problem.

  2. 2

    Scoop out the suds

    Bail the foam into a bucket and dump it outside. Suds in a machine can be cleared by running a cold rinse cycle with no soap.

  3. 3

    Flush with hot tap water

    Run the hot tap for a few minutes. If it drains, grease was the issue and the line is clear for now.

  4. 4

    Try a plunger on one fixture

    Use a cup plunger on a sink and block the other side if it is a double bowl. Skip chemical drain cleaners.

  5. 5

    Call if it comes back

    A repeat slow drain means grease or scale is built up farther down the line. A cable or hydro jetting will clear what a plunger cannot.

Soap, disposals and dishwashers

A disposal shreds food and sends it into the drain along with whatever grease came with it. Dish soap in the disposal makes a foam that pushes up the sink. Use cold water, run it a few seconds before and after, and skip the grease.

Dishwashers want only detergent made for them. Hand dish soap in a machine produces a mountain of suds, which can leak from the door and soak the floor. If that has happened, a plumber can check the drain hose and the high loop under the sink. Our garbage disposal repair page covers units that jam or leak.

Check your dishwasher maker's manufacturer guidelines for the detergent it calls for, because hand dish soap is not made for machines.
