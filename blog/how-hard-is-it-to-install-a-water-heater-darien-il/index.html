How Hard Is It to Install a Water Heater in Darien, IL?

Hooking up two pipes sounds simple. The hard parts are gas, venting, the relief valve and local rules, and they are where mistakes become dangerous.

How hard is a water heater installation?

Swapping a water heater is moderately hard for a trained plumber and risky for most homeowners. The water connections are the easy part. The hard parts are gas piping, venting exhaust safely, setting the relief valve and drain correctly and meeting local rules. Mistakes in those areas can cause carbon monoxide, gas leaks or scalding.

Illinois requires a license for anyone who installs, alters or repairs plumbing. The City of Darien requires permits whenever plumbing is changed, and does not list water heaters on its permit page, so ask Community Development at 1702 Plainfield Rd what applies.

Below we break the job into steps so you can see where the risk sits. A correct install follows manufacturer guidelines for clearances and venting, along with the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code).

Each part of the job, rated by difficulty and risk

The difficulty depends on the unit. Electric heaters have fewer risks than gas.

TaskDifficultyWhat can go wrong
Draining and removing the old tankModerate, heavy liftingScalding, flooding, back injury
Water connections and shutoff valvesEasy to moderateLeaks at fittings, wrong connectors
Gas line and shutoffHighGas leak, fire, explosion
Venting and draftHighCarbon monoxide entering the house
Temperature and pressure relief valve and drain lineModerateScalding discharge, no safety relief
Electric wiring for an electric unitModerate to highShock, fire, wrong breaker size
Expansion tank where neededModeratePressure spikes, relief valve dripping

Never work on gas piping yourself. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility.

The gas and venting problem

A gas water heater burns fuel and must send exhaust outside. The vent needs the right size, slope and clearances, and the heater needs enough air to burn cleanly. A poor vent can let carbon monoxide spill into the house, and you cannot see or smell it.

Gas connections must be leak tested with the proper method. Never use a flame to check. If you smell gas at any time, leave, call 911 and do not use light switches.

This is why we do not recommend do-it-yourself gas heater work. Our water heater installation page lists the connections and checks a plumber completes.

What a professional install looks like

  1. 1

    Check the old setup

    The plumber reads the old unit, checks the vent, gas line, shutoffs and available space.

  2. 2

    Drain and remove

    Power or gas is shut off, the tank is drained and the old unit is disconnected and carried out.

  3. 3

    Set and connect

    The new unit is set, water lines and shutoffs are connected, and the relief valve and drain are piped.

  4. 4

    Connect fuel and vent

    Gas or electric connections are made, the vent is sized and sealed, and joints are leak tested.

  5. 5

    Fill, light and test

    The tank is filled before power is on, the burner is lit or the elements are energized and combustion and temperature are checked.

Darien basements, fire district and the first minutes after a gas smell

Most Darien water heaters sit in basements or utility closets in houses built from the 1960s onward. Those spaces are tight, and older vents and gas shutoffs may have aged. Check the vent connection and the date on the existing unit before you plan a replacement.

If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call 911. Darien is served by the Darien-Woodridge Fire Protection District at 7550 Lyman Ave, and Tri-State Fire Protection District covers part of the city east of Cass Ave and north of I-55. Calling 911 reaches the right one.

Darien buys Lake Michigan water from the City of Chicago. The City does not publish a hardness figure, so test your tap or call (630) 353-8105. Mineral buildup affects tank life and is a fair question to raise with your installer.

What affects the cost of a water heater installation

The unit is one part of the price. The setup around it changes the rest. You hear a clear price before work starts.

Fuel typeGas, electric and hybrid units each need different connections.
Tank sizeLarger tanks cost more to buy and heavier to move.
Vent conditionAn old or undersized vent may need replacement.
Code updatesRelief valve piping, shutoffs, a drain pan or an expansion tank may need to be added.
Location and accessStairs, tight closets and long carry distances add time.
PermitAsk Community Development whether a permit applies to your job.
Homeowners insurance usually does not pay to replace a water heater that failed from age. Sudden water damage from a burst tank may be covered depending on your policy, so photograph the damage before cleanup.

What to ask before you hire an installer

Ask whether the installer holds an Illinois plumbing license. Illinois offers a public license verification tool. Ask what will be done with the old vent, who handles the permit question and what the work includes, such as new shutoffs and a relief valve line.
