Where Willowbrook's water comes from and why it matters at the tap

The Village of Willowbrook says all of its daily drinking water comes from Lake Michigan through the DuPage Water Commission, averaging about 1 million gallons a day. The Village keeps a deep well as an emergency backup. The 2025 water quality report adds that water passes through a storage standpipe and pumping station before reaching homes.

That means a brief pressure dip or cloudy spell is often a system event, not a pipe failure inside your house. Check with a neighbor and the Village first. If only one fixture or one floor is affected, the cause is likely your plumbing, and that is when you call us.

The report also suggests flushing a tap for 30 seconds to 2 minutes when water has sat for several hours. Use that same habit to clear stale water before you judge a faucet or a water heater by its first draw.
