Why pipes split in Northern Illinois winters

Water expands as it turns to ice. A frozen section of pipe does not usually burst where the ice sits. Pressure builds between the ice plug and a closed faucet, and the pipe splits in the weak spot.

That is why a pipe can look fine while frozen and flood the house after it thaws. Copper, steel and plastic all split under pressure. The split often shows as a lengthwise crack, a bulge or a blown fitting.

Frozen pipe thaw: what is safe and what is not

A pipe that is frozen but not split can sometimes be thawed with care. A pipe that has already split must be repaired, because thawing it only starts the flood.

What a homeowner can do

Open the nearest faucet so melting water and steam have a way out. Warm the room with a space heater placed away from anything flammable, or use a hair dryer on low and keep moving it along the pipe. Start at the end closest to the faucet and work back toward the frozen part.

What to never do

Never use an open flame, torch or kerosene heater on a pipe. Fire and super-heated pipe are the common causes of damage after a thaw. Do not leave heat on a pipe unattended.

When to call right away

If a pipe is inside a wall, under a slab or against an outside wall you cannot reach, call us. A plumber can thaw it with controlled heat, check for cracks, and shut off the line before anything pours out.

Older Darien homes and where pipes freeze

Darien incorporated in 1969 around the Marion Hills, Brookhaven, Farmingdale and Hinsbrook subdivisions, so many homes in the city date from the 1960s and later. Homes of that period often have supply lines running along exterior walls, in garages and through unheated crawl areas.

Practical takeaway: after a freeze, check those spots first. Look at the lines near the garage, along north-facing walls and where pipes enter the house. If one has split once, ask for a look at nearby lines, because a second weak spot may be close.

Repair choices after a pipe bursts

Once the water is off, the plumber looks at how much pipe is damaged. A short split can be cut out and replaced with a new section. A fitting that blew apart is replaced along with the pipe next to it.

If several sections are damaged or the old piping is brittle, we may suggest a bigger replacement. Our whole house repiping page explains when that makes sense. For a break between the meter and the house, water service line repair covers the buried run. Replacement pipe and fittings are chosen to match the standards in the Illinois Plumbing Code (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code) and the pipe maker's manufacturer guidelines.

After the water stops: drying and damage

Wet drywall, insulation and carpet start to grow mold in a short time, so removing standing water and drying the area matters. A plumber stops the source and repairs the pipe. Water removal, drywall and flooring are separate jobs for restoration companies.

Keep photos and notes for your insurance claim. If a leak is slow or hidden after repairs, our leak detection service can check for water still escaping.
